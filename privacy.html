Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This policy explains what happens to your data when you use this site and the tools linked from it. It's written in plain language on purpose.

Tools run in your browser

The tools listed on this site process files (PDFs, images, text) directly in your browser. Unless a specific tool's own page says otherwise, your files are not uploaded to a server and are never seen or stored by us.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. More detail on how Google uses data is available at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand which tools are useful, such as anonymized page-view counts. No personally identifying information is collected or sold. Third-party services embedded on this site (including advertising) may set their own cookies, governed by their own privacy policies.

Third-party links

Some tools link out to their own repositories or pages. Once you leave this site, its privacy practices no longer apply — check the destination site's own policy.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as tools are added. Material changes will update the date above.
